Project Title

Medium / Format

Dusk Blue, the Networks of the Unseen, is a pop-up exhibition exploring technology, ecology, memory, and the unseen systems that govern our reality. Dusk Blue is the nautical term for the final moment of twilight before total darkness. Within this faint blue light, sailors make their navigational calibrations. Similarly, this work in progress exhibition captures 37 artists at a vital threshold: the final, visible moments of error code correction, material experimentation, and conceptual adjustment before a final degree show.

Rafa Roeder
